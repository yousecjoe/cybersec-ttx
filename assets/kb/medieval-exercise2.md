# The Alchemist's Secret: Shadow in the Guild
## A Medieval-Themed Cybersecurity Tabletop Exercise

## Exercise Overview

**Title:** The Alchemist's Secret: Shadow in the Guild  
**Duration:** 4 hours (recommended)  
**Target Audience:** Guild Masters, Alchemist Apprentices, City Watch, Merchant Council  
**Difficulty:** Apprentice to Master-level  
**Objective:** Test the Alchemist Guild's ability to detect, contain, and neutralize a sophisticated plot to steal secret formulas and sabotage legitimate potions, threatening both guild reputation and public safety.

## Learning Objectives

1. Evaluate team coordination during complex security breaches within the guild system
2. Test response capabilities across different types of alchemical tampering
3. Assess communication protocols between guilds and city authorities
4. Practice decision-making under pressure with potential citywide health consequences
5. Identify gaps in current security measures for protecting trade secrets and formulas

## Exercise Structure

### Preparation Phase (2 weeks prior)

1. **Guildmaster Selection:** Appoint 1-2 individuals to coordinate the exercise
2. **Guild Member Selection:** Identify key personnel from various ranks and specialties
3. **Resource Preparation:** Ready the necessary documentation, secure potion samples, and simulated laboratory environment
4. **Pre-Exercise Briefing:** Conduct a guild meeting explaining exercise parameters and expectations

### Exercise Roles

1. **Guildmaster:** Controls exercise flow, introduces scenarios, evaluates responses
2. **Master Alchemists:** Senior personnel responsible for formula verification and authentication
3. **Journeymen Apothecaries:** Mid-level specialists who test and analyze suspicious potions
4. **Apprentice Investigators:** Guild members who gather evidence and conduct initial assessments
5. **Merchant Council:** Decision-makers who balance guild integrity with market concerns
6. **Observers:** Record actions, decisions, and potential improvements
7. **City Watch Representatives:** (Optional) Add realism with official authority involvement

## Exercise Materials

### Required Documentation

1. Guild security protocols
2. Emergency response procedures

5. Registry of master alchemists and their signature techniques
6. Exercise evaluation metrics

### Technical Setup (Optional)

## Scenario Background

The Royal Alchemist Guild serves as the kingdom's premier organization for the creation, verification, and distribution of medicinal potions, magical elixirs, and industrial compounds. The Guild's Security Division provides monitoring and protection for:

- The Grand Formulary (repository of secret recipes)
- Ingredient Supply Chain (quality and authenticity verification)
- Apprentice Certification Process (preventing unqualified practitioners)
- Market Monitoring System (identifying counterfeit products)

The Guild utilizes an intricate system of master-apprentice verification, with multiple layers of secrecy and compartmentalized knowledge to protect their most valuable formulas.

## Exercise Narrative

A rival organization, the "Shadowvial Cabal," has initiated a complex plot against the Alchemist Guild. Led by a former guild member expelled for ethical violations, the Cabal aims to steal valuable formulas, introduce counterfeit potions to the market, and subtly sabotage legitimate remedies to undermine public trust in the guild. The attack will unfold in multiple stages over the course of the exercise, combining formula theft, ingredient tampering, and infiltration of the guild's apprentice ranks.

## Exercise Timeline and Scenarios

### Phase 1: Initial Detection (0:00-1:00)

**Setting the Scene (0:00-0:10)**
- Guildmaster introduces the scenario as a normal day at the Alchemist Guild
- Teams are performing routine duties in their respective roles

**Scenario 1 (0:10): Unusual Customer Reaction**
- A city merchant reports multiple customers experiencing unexpected side effects from standard healing potions
- The potions bear proper guild seals and packaging
- Effects are mild but noticeably different from normal product performance

*Expected Actions:*
- Retrieve samples of the affected potions
- Verify batch numbers and production details
- Begin preliminary testing of potion contents
- Document the incident in the guild registry

**Scenario 2 (0:30): Ingredient Discrepancy**
- Quarterly inventory reveals unusual depletion rates of rare ingredients
- Usage records don't account for the missing materials
- The missing ingredients are components of several valuable proprietary formulas

*Expected Actions:*
- Escalate the incident to senior guild officials
- Review access logs to the ingredient storage areas
- Begin tracking pattern of ingredients to identify targeted formulas
- Start assembling an investigation team

**Scenario 3 (0:45): Apprentice Behavior Report**
- A senior alchemist reports an apprentice working during unauthorized hours
- When confronted, the apprentice claimed to be practicing standard formulas
- Later inspection of the workspace reveals unusual equipment configurations

*Expected Actions:*
- Activate formal security incident protocols
- Secure relevant workspaces and materials
- Begin investigation into the apprentice's activities
- Consider restricting access to high-value ingredients and formulas

### Phase 2: Escalation (1:00-2:00)

**Scenario 4 (1:00): Market Infiltration**
- The City Watch reports confiscating counterfeit potions from a street vendor
- The potions bear convincing guild markings but contain diluted ingredients
- The vendor claims to have acquired them from a legitimate guild supplier

*Expected Actions:*
- Acknowledge potential connection to earlier incidents
- Collaborate with City Watch investigators
- Inspect the counterfeit guild seals for forensic clues
- Update incident documentation and escalate to guild leadership

**Scenario 5 (1:20): Formula Alteration Discovery**
- Routine reference to the Grand Formulary reveals subtle modifications to a valuable healing elixir
- The changes would cause the reported side effects if implemented
- The modifications appear to have been made during legitimate access sessions

*Expected Actions:*
- Perform detailed analysis of all potentially modified formulas
- Begin investigating the authentication logs for the Formulary
- Compare formula changes with the missing ingredient pattern
- Update response team and Merchant Council

**Scenario 6 (1:40): Evidence of Coordinated Effort**
- Investigation reveals communication between the suspicious apprentice and external parties
- Coded messages reference delivery schedules and formula details
- A pattern emerges suggesting multiple infiltrators within the guild

*Expected Actions:*
- Expand investigation to identify all potential collaborators
- Preserve evidence for potential prosecution
- Implement enhanced monitoring of all apprentice activities
- Update risk assessment based on potential organizational infiltration

### Phase 3: Crisis Management (2:00-3:00)

**Scenario 7 (2:00): Public Health Concern**
- Reports arrive of serious illness from compromised potions in a noble household
- The affected batch contains harmful contaminants not detected in initial testing
- The noble family threatens to publicly denounce the guild

*Expected Actions:*
- Prioritize treatment for affected individuals
- Implement emergency recall of suspected potion batches
- Deploy master alchemists to develop counteragents
- Prepare for potential public health crisis

**Scenario 8 (2:20): Royal Involvement**
- The King's Physician demands immediate explanation of the contamination
- Reports indicate potentially compromised potions in the royal medicine cabinet
- Rumors of guild negligence begin spreading through the city

*Expected Actions:*
- Prepare concise briefing with verified information
- Propose immediate countermeasures to secure royal safety
- Advise on public communications strategy
- Continue investigation while addressing noble concerns

**Scenario 9 (2:40): Sabotage Revelation**
- A journeyman alchemist discovers evidence of systematic tampering
- Critical quality control runes have been subtly altered throughout the guild
- A sealed message is found threatening widespread poisoning unless paid in gold

*Expected Actions:*
- Document the full extent of tampering
- Implement emergency quality verification procedures
- Discuss response options with guild leadership
- Prepare for escalated threats to public safety
- Consider involving royal authorities more extensively

### Phase 4: Resolution and Recovery (3:00-4:00)

**Scenario 10 (3:00): Shadowvial Cabal Identified**
- Intelligence from the City Watch identifies the rival organization
- Evidence confirms the former guild member's leadership role
- Analysis reveals their methodology for compromising guild security

*Expected Actions:*
- Document complete findings for authorities
- Develop a comprehensive remediation plan for all compromised areas
- Identify critical security vulnerabilities requiring immediate attention
- Prepare technical briefing on formula and ingredient verification

**Scenario 11 (3:20): Containment Decision Point**
- The response team must decide on final containment strategy
- Options include public recall of all recent potions vs. targeted recovery of only compromised batches
- Each option balances different risks to public health and guild reputation

*Expected Actions:*
- Evaluate public safety and reputational impacts
- Make decisions based on comprehensive risk assessment
- Communicate decisions and rationale to all stakeholders
- Begin implementing the chosen strategy

**Scenario 12 (3:40): Recovery Planning**
- With immediate threats addressed, focus shifts to restoring guild reputation
- Procedures for verifying uncompromised formulas must be established
- The public and nobility require reassurance about potion safety

*Expected Actions:*
- Develop a prioritized recovery sequence
- Create a communication plan for different stakeholders
- Prepare preliminary announcements for public distribution
- Begin documenting lessons learned for guild protocols

### Conclusion (3:50-4:00)

- Guildmaster declares the end of the exercise
- Brief initial feedback from participants
- Schedule a formal debrief session for the following day

## Exercise Evaluation

### Evaluation Metrics

1. **Detection Effectiveness**
   - Time to detect initial anomalies
   - Ability to correlate related security events
   - Thoroughness of investigation

2. **Response Efficiency**
   - Time from detection to initial response
   - Appropriateness of response actions
   - Resource allocation and expert deployment decisions

3. **Communication Effectiveness**
   - Internal guild communication clarity and timeliness
   - Coordination with city authorities
   - Council updates and escalations

4. **Decision Quality**
   - Public safety prioritization
   - Decision-making balancing formula protection with transparency
   - Balance between security and continued potion production

### Post-Exercise Activities

1. **Initial Council (Immediately following exercise)**
   - Quick round-table discussion of initial impressions
   - Identification of major strengths and challenges
   - Collection of immediate feedback

2. **Formal Guild Review (1-2 days after exercise)**
   - Structured review of exercise timeline and decisions
   - Analysis of major decision points
   - Documentation of lessons learned

3. **Improvement Planning (1-2 weeks after exercise)**
   - Development of specific action items
   - Assignment of responsibilities for improvements
   - Timeline for implementing changes

4. **Follow-up Exercise (3-6 months later)**
   - Targeted scenario to test improvements
   - Focus on previously identified weaknesses
   - Validate effectiveness of changes

## Guildmaster Guidelines

### Pre-Exercise Preparation

1. **Scenario Customization**
   - Adjust alchemical details to match your guild's specific formulas
   - Modify cabal capabilities as appropriate for difficulty level
   - Ensure scenarios are realistic for available personnel and resources

2. **Information Control**
   - Determine what information is available to participants at each stage
   - Prepare answers for likely questions from participants
   - Create physical or digital information cards for scenarios

3. **Environment Setup**
   - Arrange the exercise space to simulate guild operations
   - Prepare physical props (potion bottles, ingredient samples, etc.)
   - Consider appropriate costumes and room layout

### During Exercise Facilitation

1. **Maintaining Alchemical Realism**
   - Introduce complications that would challenge medieval scientists
   - Provide realistic time pressures for potion-related emergencies
   - Balance formula secrecy with period-appropriate techniques

2. **Adaptability**
   - Be prepared to adjust scenario pacing based on participant progress
   - Have additional alchemical challenges ready if teams resolve issues quickly
   - Be willing to provide hints if teams get completely stuck

3. **Observation**
   - Take notes on key decisions and actions
   - Identify teaching moments for the debrief
   - Document specific areas for improvement

### Post-Exercise Activities

1. **Facilitating Discussion**
   - Use open-ended questions to promote reflection
   - Focus on process improvements rather than assigning blame
   - Highlight both strengths and areas for improvement

2. **Documentation**
   - Compile observations and participant feedback
   - Prepare a comprehensive after-action report
   - Develop specific, actionable recommendations

## Appendix: Detailed Technical Scenarios

### Technical Details for Scenario 1
- Affected Product: Standard Healing Draught, Batch #437
- Reported Effects: Mild drowsiness, blue tongue discoloration, accelerated healing but with slight fever
- Packaging: Correct guild seal, proper labeling, authentic glass vials
- Distribution: Sold through Goodwife Apothecary, a licensed guild vendor
- Normal Effects: Pain relief and accelerated healing without side effects

### Technical Details for Scenario 2
- Missing Ingredients:
  - Moonflower petals (7 drams more than recorded usage)
  - Powdered basilisk scale (approximately 2 ounces unaccounted for)
  - Distilled morning dew (3 vials missing)
  - Black lotus extract (1 vial, highly regulated substance)
  - Dragon's blood resin (small but noticeable amount)
- Access Records: All registered guild members with proper authorization
- Common Usage: These ingredients collectively appear in only 5 high-value formulas

### Technical Details for Scenario 3
- Apprentice Details:
  - Name: Cedric Thistlewick
  - Guild Tenure: 7 months (relatively new)
  - Assigned Master: Alchemist Bartholomew (currently traveling)
  - Unauthorized Activity: Working at midnight, typically forbidden
  - Workspace Anomalies: Unusual distillation setup, non-standard ingredient combinations, hidden notebook

### Technical Details for Scenario 5
- Formula Alterations:
  - Original: "Add three drops of willow essence after cooling"
  - Modified: "Add three drops of willow essence while still warm"
  - Effect of Change: Creates mild toxin when willow essence overheats
  - Authentication: Changes made using legitimate access credentials
  - Pattern: Similar subtle but dangerous modifications to four other formulas
  - Verification Method: Changes only detectable by comparing to archived copies

### Technical Details for Scenario 7
- Contamination Details:
  - Affected Family: House Wellerton (close advisors to the king)
  - Symptoms: High fever, discoloration of skin, temporary paralysis
  - Batch Identification: Sleeping draught, Batch #442
  - Contaminant: Nightshade extract, not part of normal formula
  - Detection Difficulty: Masked by lavender scent, standard in the potion
  - Potential Outcome: Non-lethal but serious illness requiring specialized treatment

### Technical Details for Scenario 9
- Sabotage Evidence:
  - Altered Quality Runes: Inspection symbols modified to pass substandard materials
  - Control Process: Temperature regulation runes subtly changed to allow improper brewing conditions
  - Distribution: Inspection stamps for market-ready potions compromised
  - Threat Message:
    ```
    TO THE ALCHEMIST GUILD MASTERS:
    
    Your precious potions now serve our will. We control what flows from your guild to the lips of the city.
    
    The effects thus far are merely a demonstration. Without our antidote, the hidden components in your
    distributed potions will activate within a fortnight, bringing suffering throughout the realm.
    
    Deliver 5,000 gold crowns to the abandoned mill beyond North Gate by the next full moon.
    Include the complete formula for the Elixir of Restoration.
    
    Defy us, and watch your guild's reputation die along with your customers.
    
    - SHADOWVIAL
    ```

### Technical Details for Scenario 10
- Infiltration Methodology:
  1. Initial access through falsified apprentice credentials
  2. Collection of guild procedures and security protocols
  3. Gradual theft of small amounts of rare ingredients
  4. Modification of formulas during legitimate access sessions
  5. Introduction of counterfeit products through compromised supply chain
  6. Subtle alteration of quality control processes to allow tampering
  7. Coordination through coded messages disguised as standard recipes
