# The Royal Seal: Whispers of Treason
## A Medieval-Themed Cybersecurity Tabletop Exercise

## Exercise Overview

**Title:** The Royal Seal: Whispers of Treason  
**Duration:** 4 hours (recommended)  
**Target Audience:** Royal Scribes, King's Messengers, Court Spymasters, Noble Council  
**Difficulty:** Squire to Knight-level  
**Objective:** Test the Royal Court's ability to detect, contain, and neutralize a sophisticated plot to infiltrate the kingdom's message system and forge royal documents to destabilize the realm.

## Learning Objectives

1. Evaluate team coordination during complex security incidents in the royal court
2. Test response capabilities across different forms of message tampering
3. Assess communication protocols between castle staff and outlying vassals
4. Practice decision-making under pressure with potential kingdom-wide consequences
5. Identify gaps in current security measures for royal communications

## Exercise Structure

### Preparation Phase (2 weeks prior)

1. **Lord Chancellor Selection:** Appoint 1-2 individuals to coordinate the exercise
2. **Court Official Selection:** Identify key personnel from various royal departments
3. **Resource Preparation:** Ready the necessary scrolls, secure messenger routes, and simulated royal chambers
4. **Pre-Exercise Briefing:** Conduct a privy council meeting explaining exercise parameters and expectations

### Exercise Roles

1. **Lord Chancellor:** Controls exercise flow, introduces scenarios, evaluates responses
2. **Royal Scribes:** Personnel responsible for document creation and verification
3. **King's Messengers:** Couriers who ensure secure delivery of communications
4. **Court Spymasters:** Intelligence specialists who investigate threats
5. **Noble Council:** Decision-makers who balance security with kingdom governance
6. **Observers:** Record actions, decisions, and potential improvements
7. **Allied Kingdom Representatives:** (Optional) Add realism with international diplomacy

## Exercise Materials

### Required Documentation

1. Kingdom security protocols

4. Incident escalation matrices
5. Registry of official seals and signatures
6. Exercise evaluation metrics

### Technical Setup (Optional)

## Scenario Background

The Royal Court operates the King's Chancery, responsible for creating, protecting, and distributing official communications throughout the realm. The Court's Royal Security Division provides monitoring and protection for:

- The Great Seal of the Realm (authenticity verification)
- Royal Messenger Network (secure communications)
- Royal Archives (document storage and history)
- Kingdom Watch System (threat monitoring across borders)

The Court utilizes a sophisticated network of scribes and messengers, with the King's Spymaster providing intelligence on potential threats to the security of royal communications.

## Exercise Narrative

A shadowy group calling themselves "The Faceless Brotherhood" has identified the royal communication system as their target for destabilizing the kingdom. Led by a disgraced former royal scribe with inside knowledge, the group aims to intercept messages, forge royal decrees, and spread false orders that will turn nobles against each other and weaken the realm for invasion. The plot will unfold in multiple stages over the course of the exercise, combining forgery, bribery, and infiltration of the royal messenger system.

## Exercise Timeline and Scenarios

### Phase 1: Initial Detection (0:00-1:00)

**Setting the Scene (0:00-0:10)**
- Lord Chancellor introduces the scenario as a normal day at court
- Teams are performing routine duties in their respective roles

**Scenario 1 (0:10): Unusual Messenger Behavior**
- A castle guard reports a royal messenger taking an unauthorized route
- The messenger carried official documents but avoided the normal checkpoints
- When questioned later, the messenger claimed to be following special instructions

*Expected Actions:*
- Investigate the messenger's story
- Verify the documents carried were legitimate
- Review messenger assignment records
- Begin documenting the incident in the court ledger

**Scenario 2 (0:30): Seal Wax Discrepancy**
- A routine inspection identifies subtle differences in the wax used on recent royal documents
- The color and texture vary slightly from the official royal wax
- Documents with the unusual wax contain routine orders to outlying garrisons

*Expected Actions:*
- Escalate the incident to senior court officials
- Compare the questionable documents with known authentic ones
- Begin tracing the origin of the altered wax
- Start assembling an investigation team

**Scenario 3 (0:45): Message Interception Report**
- A loyal baron sends word that he received conflicting orders from the king
- The second message arrived via an unfamiliar messenger
- The content contradicted previous instructions regarding tax collection

*Expected Actions:*
- Activate formal security incident protocols
- Secure all official seals and document materials
- Begin investigation into document origination
- Consider notifying key nobles about potential forgeries

### Phase 2: Escalation (1:00-2:00)

**Scenario 4 (1:00): Border Checkpoint Alert**
- The Northern Watch reports detaining a traveler with partial royal messenger insignia
- The individual carried no messages but possessed scraps of official parchment
- When questioned, the person claimed to be a messenger trainee

*Expected Actions:*
- Acknowledge potential connection to earlier incidents
- Dispatch investigators to interrogate the detained individual
- Check royal messenger training records
- Update incident documentation and escalate to higher authority

**Scenario 5 (1:20): Forgery Workshop Discovery**
- A castle servant reports unusual activities in an abandoned tower
- Investigation reveals equipment for document creation and seal forgery
- Evidence suggests recent use and hasty abandonment

*Expected Actions:*
- Perform detailed analysis of the discovered materials
- Begin investigating how the space was accessed undetected
- Compare forged seals with the suspicious documents previously identified
- Update response team and noble council

**Scenario 6 (1:40): Evidence of Insider Assistance**
- Investigation reveals a junior scribe has been providing information to unknown parties
- The scribe's quarters contain notes on messenger routes and guard schedules
- The scribe is currently missing from assigned duties

*Expected Actions:*
- Initiate kingdom-wide alert for the missing scribe
- Preserve evidence for further investigation
- Determine what sensitive information may have been compromised
- Update risk assessment based on potential information exposure

### Phase 3: Crisis Management (2:00-3:00)

**Scenario 7 (2:00): Vassal Loyalty Questions**
- Baron Westfield arrives demanding explanation for orders to attack a neighboring ally
- He presents a document bearing the royal seal commanding military action
- The document appears authentic but the orders were never issued by the king

*Expected Actions:*
- Prioritize preventing military action based on false orders
- Implement emergency communication to affected vassals
- Deploy court representatives to critical locations
- Prepare for potential diplomatic incidents

**Scenario 8 (2:20): King's Involvement**
- The king demands immediate briefing on the security situation
- Reports arrive of confusion among multiple noble houses
- Rumors of royal instability begin spreading through the capital

*Expected Actions:*
- Prepare concise briefing with verified information
- Propose immediate countermeasures to restore order
- Advise on public communications strategy
- Continue investigation while addressing noble concerns

**Scenario 9 (2:40): Ransom Demand**
- A message arrives from The Faceless Brotherhood
- They demonstrate knowledge of genuine state secrets
- They demand gold and political concessions to prevent further chaos

*Expected Actions:*
- Document the full contents of the ransom demand
- Assess how they obtained the referenced state secrets
- Discuss response options with the royal council
- Prepare for escalated disruption of kingdom communications
- Consider deploying elite guards to protect key messengers and documents

### Phase 4: Resolution and Recovery (3:00-4:00)

**Scenario 10 (3:00): Brotherhood Headquarters Located**
- Intelligence pinpoints the location of the forgery operation
- Evidence confirms The Faceless Brotherhood's involvement
- Analysis reveals their methodology for penetrating royal security

*Expected Actions:*
- Document complete findings for the King's Guard
- Develop a prioritized plan to secure all communication channels
- Identify critical security vulnerabilities requiring immediate attention
- Prepare tactical options for raiding the Brotherhood's headquarters

**Scenario 11 (3:20): Containment Decision Point**
- The response team must decide on final containment strategy
- Options include immediate raid on the Brotherhood headquarters vs. disinformation campaign
- Each option balances different risks to personnel and kingdom stability

*Expected Actions:*
- Evaluate offensive and defensive options
- Make decisions based on comprehensive risk assessment
- Communicate decisions and rationale to all team members
- Begin implementing the chosen strategy

**Scenario 12 (3:40): Recovery Planning**
- With immediate threats addressed, focus shifts to restoring trust in royal communications
- Methods for verifying legitimate orders must be established
- Noble houses require reassurance of kingdom stability

*Expected Actions:*
- Develop a prioritized recovery sequence
- Create a communication plan for different vassals and allies
- Prepare preliminary statements for court announcement
- Begin documenting lessons learned for court protocols

### Conclusion (3:50-4:00)

- Lord Chancellor declares the end of the exercise
- Brief initial feedback from participants
- Schedule a formal debrief session for the following day

## Exercise Evaluation

### Evaluation Metrics

1. **Detection Effectiveness**
   - Time to detect initial anomalies
   - Ability to correlate related security events
   - Thoroughness of investigation

2. **Response Efficiency**
   - Time from detection to initial response
   - Appropriateness of response actions
   - Resource allocation and messenger deployment decisions

3. **Communication Effectiveness**
   - Internal court communication security and timeliness
   - Coordination with outlying nobles
   - Council updates and escalations

4. **Decision Quality**
   - Kingdom stability prioritization
   - Decision-making balancing offensive and defensive measures
   - Balance between security and continued governance

### Post-Exercise Activities

1. **Initial Council (Immediately following exercise)**
   - Quick round-table discussion of initial impressions
   - Identification of major strengths and challenges
   - Collection of immediate feedback

2. **Formal Court Review (1-2 days after exercise)**
   - Structured review of exercise timeline and decisions
   - Analysis of major decision points
   - Documentation of lessons learned

3. **Improvement Planning (1-2 weeks after exercise)**
   - Development of specific action items
   - Assignment of responsibilities for improvements
   - Timeline for implementing changes

4. **Follow-up Exercise (3-6 months later)**
   - Targeted scenario to test improvements
   - Focus on previously identified weaknesses
   - Validate effectiveness of changes

## Lord Chancellor Guidelines

### Pre-Exercise Preparation

1. **Scenario Customization**
   - Adjust details to match your court's specific procedures
   - Modify brotherhood capabilities as appropriate for difficulty level
   - Ensure scenarios are realistic for available personnel and resources

2. **Information Control**
   - Determine what information is available to participants at each stage
   - Prepare answers for likely questions from participants
   - Create physical or digital information cards for scenarios

3. **Environment Setup**
   - Arrange the exercise space to simulate court operations
   - Prepare physical props (scrolls, seals, etc.)
   - Consider appropriate costumes and room layout

### During Exercise Facilitation

1. **Maintaining Medieval Realism**
   - Introduce complications that would challenge royal officials
   - Provide realistic time pressures for kingdom-level threats
   - Balance document security with period-appropriate techniques

2. **Adaptability**
   - Be prepared to adjust scenario pacing based on participant progress
   - Have additional challenges ready if teams resolve issues quickly
   - Be willing to provide hints if teams get completely stuck

3. **Observation**
   - Take notes on key decisions and actions
   - Identify teaching moments for the debrief
   - Document specific areas for improvement

### Post-Exercise Activities

1. **Facilitating Discussion**
   - Use open-ended questions to promote reflection
   - Focus on process improvements rather than assigning blame
   - Highlight both strengths and areas for improvement

2. **Documentation**
   - Compile observations and participant feedback
   - Prepare a comprehensive after-action report
   - Develop specific, actionable recommendations

## Appendix: Detailed Technical Scenarios

### Technical Details for Scenario 1
- Messenger Name: Cedric of Oakvale
- Unauthorized Route: Avoided the Eastern Guard Tower checkpoint
- Documents Carried: Routine garrison supply orders for Castle Blackwood
- Explanation When Questioned: "Received verbal orders from Master Scribe Thomas to use the merchant's path for speed"
- Verification: Master Scribe Thomas denies giving such instructions

### Technical Details for Scenario 2
- Seal Discrepancies:
  - Color: Slightly more orange-red than the true crimson of royal wax
  - Texture: Lacks the fine gold flecks present in authentic royal wax
  - Impression: Seal depth approximately 0.5mm shallower than standard
  - Age: Wax appears to crack differently when folded compared to authentic documents
  - Affected Documents: Primarily routine orders to outlying garrisons regarding patrol schedules

### Technical Details for Scenario 3
- Conflicting Orders:
  ```
  Original Order: "Maintain current tax collection schedule as established in spring council."
  Counterfeit Order: "Double tax collection immediately to fund border defenses against imminent threat."
  ```
- Delivery Details: Second message arrived via rider claiming urgent status
- Authentication Issues: Seal appears correct but the king's signature shows subtle differences
- Potential Impact: Civil unrest if higher taxes implemented based on false orders

### Technical Details for Scenario 5
- Forgery Workshop Contents:
  - Partial royal seal cast in bronze
  - Various wax samples with color additives
  - High-quality parchment matching royal supplies
  - Quills and inks matching court standards
  - Practice documents with attempted signature imitations
  - Notes on messenger schedules and routes
  - Inventory of orders successfully forged and distributed

### Technical Details for Scenario 7
- Forged Military Order:
  - Addressed to: Baron Westfield of the Northern Marches
  - Content: Commands immediate mobilization against Earl Sutherland (a longtime ally)
  - Justification: Claims evidence of Sutherland's conspiracy with foreign powers
  - Authentication: Bears correct seal impression and convincing royal signature
  - Delivery: Arrived via standard royal messenger through proper channels

### Technical Details for Scenario 9
- Brotherhood Ransom Demand:
  ```
  TO HIS ROYAL MAJESTY'S COUNCIL:
  
  Your kingdom's words are now ours to command.
  
  We have demonstrated our ability to speak with the King's voice.
  As proof, we offer knowledge that only the inner council should possess:
  The secret marriage negotiations with Eastmark, the hidden northern treasury, 
  and the King's private agreement with the Merchant Guild.
  
  Our demands are simple:
  1. 10,000 gold crowns delivered to the location our agent will specify
  2. Full pardon for Lord Xavier and restoration of his lands
  3. Withdrawal of royal forces from the Western Provinces

  - THE FACELESS BROTHERHOOD
  ```

### Technical Details for Scenario 10
- Operation Methodology:
  1. Initial access through bribed junior scribe
  2. Collection of material samples (parchment, wax, ink)
  3. Creation of forged seal and practice of royal signatures
  4. Recruitment and coercion of actual messengers for document delivery
  5. Strategic targeting of orders to create maximum disruption
  6. Use of legitimate communication channels to avoid suspicion
  7. Coordination of forgeries to systematically undermine noble trust
